Rule test · SOXL & MU · daily closes 14 Sep 2023 – 14 Sep 2026 · checked against 2010–2023

Does a −10% SOXL close mean sell everything?

Not as a timing signal. Across 54 closes at −10% or worse in the past three years, SOXL and MU did better than usual afterwards, not worse. Selling on the trigger usually meant selling just before a bounce: with a 5-day wait before buying back, MU compounded at 80% a year instead of 137%. The rule's one real use was bear-market insurance. From 2010 to 2023, standing aside for a month after each trigger cut SOXL's worst drawdown from −90% to −58%. But the cost of that insurance in bull years was large.

5 · Context

Does the kind of drop matter?

Each trigger split by its setting: trend, clustering, breadth, VIX, and whether a big chip or AI name had just reported. For a condition to be useful, the better-performing side has to be the same in both eras. Almost none pass.

One pattern worth keeping an eye on (3-year data only, 19 events): when a company beat earnings and still fell more than 5%, SOXL's median 21-day return was −2.2% and only 47% of cases were higher a month later. When earnings drops didn't look like that, 6 of 7 recovered. The sample is too small to trade on alone.

Method & caveats

How this was measured

  1. Prices. Yahoo Finance daily bars, split- and dividend-adjusted, for SOXL, MU, SOXX, NVDA, QQQ and VIX, 2010 to 14 Sep 2026. A trigger is a close-to-close SOXL return of −10% or worse. Two exchange holidays that Yahoo printed only for VIX (25 May and 7 Sep 2026) were removed.
  2. Event study. Forward returns run from the trigger close to the close 1, 5, 21 and 63 trading days later. The benchmark uses every day in the same window. “Beats X% of random samples” compares the event average with 10,000 random draws of the same number of days.
  3. Backtest. “Sell at close” assumes a market-on-close order on the trigger day. “Next open” assumes you see the close and sell at the following open. Cash earns 4% a year. There are no commissions, taxes or slippage, all of which would hurt the rule further. The placebo runs the same re-entry rule from 500 sets of random exit days.
  4. Overlap. Triggers cluster: 52% are followed by another within five days. Forward windows overlap, so the effective sample is closer to the 21 separate episodes in three years than to 54.
  5. Regime. The three-year window was an extraordinary bull run for memory (MU rose about 13×). Any rule that steps aside looks bad in it. The 2010–2023 check covers the 2011, 2015, 2018 and 2022 downturns for balance.
  6. Catalysts were labelled by hand from news searches and are marked low / medium / high confidence. Several 2026 dates had no single clear headline.
